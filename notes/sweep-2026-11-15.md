## Patterns

- **The FDA is holding the line on overall survival for PD-1×VEGF.** Ivonescimab's CRL rests only on HARMONi's non-significant OS (HR 0.79, p=0.057) despite a strong PFS. This is the first US setback for the PD-1×VEGF bispecific class that drew pharma's biggest 2025–26 bets, including AZ's $2B Summit stake six weeks ago. It will also weigh on other licensors of Chinese bispecifics.
- **Ophthalmology bispecifics licensed from China are becoming M&A targets.** Ollin was founded in 2023 around an Innovent-discovered VEGF/Ang2 bispecific, raised $330M in June and is reportedly being bought by J&J for up to $3.4B. That's an asset-centric, VC-founded (ARCH/Mubadala) company that went from in-licensing to a big-pharma exit in about 3 years. Windward (WIN378, licensed from China, IPO 11-12) follows the same model.
- **Service and infrastructure consolidation continues.** Charles River–Frontage (clinical bioanalysis) and PharmaLogic–Lantheus (a radiopharma CDMO plant with a Lu-177 supply-back deal) join MaxCyte–Akron and Waters–MOBILion from the same week. Tools, CRO and CDMO companies are buying capacity in specific niches rather than drug assets.
- **Radiopharma capacity is still scarce.** Lantheus is selling its CDMO plant while it's being acquired by Curium, but keeps supply through a long-term contract. Manufacturing is splitting off from drug ownership in this silo.
- **Tech companies:** no new life-sciences moves found in the window. Anthropic's IPO is still expected around 11-19.
