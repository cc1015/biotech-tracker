## Patterns

- **China keeps supplying the early pipeline.** Boehringer's $7B-headline option deal covers six *preclinical* programs. Western pharma is now buying Chinese ADC and T-cell engager assets before they reach Phase 1. Two of the window's three Phase 3 readouts were Chinese too (InventisBio, Gan & Lee).
- **The capacity race is about manufacturing for modalities.** Lonza's purchase targets the hardest-to-make modalities: ADC bioconjugation and payload-linkers, viral vectors and LNPs. That matches where licensing money is going (`adc`, bispecific `antibody`, `rna`).
- **Academic spinouts still lead early rounds.** Emvena (Penn Mitchell lab, via the Penn–BioNTech PxB fund) and Specific Biologics (reported as a Western University spinout, backed by Canadian venture and government funds) are both platform-plus-lead-asset companies coming out of university delivery or editing work. Neither is studio-built.
- **Obesity is moving to maintenance and less frequent dosing.** GRADUAL-3 tested whether monthly dosing holds weight after a full course, not whether it causes more loss. That is where newer GLP-1 entrants are trying to stand out.
- **Tech entrants:** nothing new in the window. Isomorphic's president gave Bloomberg interviews on 2026-11-02 and 2026-11-03 but announced no deal. DeepMind "Science Skills" / Antigravity cheminformatics coverage looked like a re-run of May and October releases, so it was not recorded.
