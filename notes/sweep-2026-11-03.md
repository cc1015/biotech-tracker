## Patterns

- **Shakeout in small and mid-cap neuro and oncology.** Four of the five market events are distress: Karyopharm bankruptcy, Acumen's only asset failing, and layoffs at Vistagen and Molecular Partners. That fits the run of cuts in late October (Artiva, LENZ, Tango). Capital keeps flowing to late-stage and platform stories (Aclaris, Rapport, Dren) while single-asset companies with weak data get wiped out.
- **Radiopharma as a refuge.** Molecular Partners is cutting nearly half its staff to focus on DARPin radioligands (MP0712), another example of companies repositioning into `radiopharma`.
- **Licensing economics are being renegotiated, not only signed.** Rhythm–LG Chem swaps an open-ended royalty for $350M fixed. Novartis returning BioArctic's BrainTransporter shows that brain-shuttle option deals are fragile even though the platform has five other partners.
- **Small AI/tools rounds outside the US.** Libo Bio (Hangzhou, AI-guided RNA-targeting small molecules) and Vitroscope (Norway, human-tissue perfusion data for drug discovery) are both platform bets, a different model from the single-asset US companies above. Neither origin (spinout vs founder-led) is confirmed.
- **Tech entrants:** quiet in this window. Isomorphic's president spoke at Bloomberg Tech on 2026-11-02 but announced no deal. The OpenAI-funded Biossil bought Theriva's antibiotic-degrading enzyme portfolio ($350K upfront; up to $91M) on 2026-11-02. It falls below the bar, so it is not in news.csv, but it is worth watching as an OpenAI-adjacent drug-asset buyer.
