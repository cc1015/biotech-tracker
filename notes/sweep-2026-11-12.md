## Patterns

- **Neuro is the hottest silo this week.** Neurosterix (schizophrenia M4 PAM), VectorY (ALS vectorized antibody) and Mentari (PACAP migraine antibodies) raised ~$680M between them. Oyster (tau degrader) and Delix earlier in the week add to it. The money is mainly going to clinical-stage single-asset or hybrid companies, not early platforms.
- **Pharma spinouts and carve-outs are getting crossover-sized rounds.** Neurosterix was carved out of Addex in 2024 and raised $105M from RTW, GV and Janus Henderson, while Addex didn't invest and fell to 8.5%. Mentari's programmes were discovered by Paragon, Fairmount's discovery engine, and came public through a reverse merger with $490M. This is the Fairmount/Paragon model of building companies around one asset.
- **Academic and venture-builder seeds stay in Europe.** Orakl (a Gustave Roussy spinout) and Neobe (from a Deep Science Ventures × Cancer Research Horizons venture programme) both sell platforms: organoid-plus-AI drug-response prediction, and engineered bacteria. Their rounds are small next to the US neuro rounds.
- **China keeps showing up on both sides.** Eli Radiopharma's ~$56M Series A is the latest Chinese radiopharma round. Vor's expanded RemeGen licence follows a familiar pattern: a small upfront ($10M) with a multibillion-dollar milestone headline.
- **Tech companies are building tools and channels, not drugs.** DeepMind shipped two science products in ten days: AlphaGenome 2, a cell-type-specific variant-effect model, and agentic cheminformatics in Antigravity, with free access for 10,000 scientists. AWS is reaching biotechs through investors, with Deerfield now following Sofinnova in June. Anthropic's IPO has slipped: reports now point to pricing ~11-18 and trading 11-19/20 at $70–78 a share. No new Microsoft, Meta, Apple, NVIDIA or OpenAI life-sciences moves in the window.
- **Tools consolidation:** MaxCyte's Akron purchase from a PE seller (Arcline) bolts reagents onto a cell-engineering platform and roughly doubles its revenue.
