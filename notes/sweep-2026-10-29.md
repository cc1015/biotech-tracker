## Patterns
- **Studios are consolidating, not only launching.** Flagship rolled three underperforming platform companies (Cellarity 2017, Empress 2020, Etiome 2021; $374M raised between them) into one AI company. The pitch is to predict clinical success rather than design molecules, and the one clinical asset (CLY-124) went into its own single-asset company. It's the platform-plus-asset-spinout pattern again. Like Formation Bio's fund (10-28) and Atlas Bio (10-27), it bets that the bottleneck is the clinic, not molecule design.
- **AI discovery money is global and early-stage.** BioGeometry (Beijing; AI-designed bispecifics plus synbio revenue) raised a state-linked A+ round led by China Life Capital. Allox (Barcelona; CRG academic spinout; mutational-scanning data + AI for allosteric sites) raised a seed led by a regional fund. Both are platforms that generate their own data.
- **Antibodies keep showing up as the AI-designed modality:** BioGeometry's bispecifics, Genmab's bispecific Phase 3 win (+33% for GMAB) and Mithril (10-27) all point the same way.
- **Commercial launches move stocks.** Replimune's +23% came from launch metrics and a narrower loss, not trial data, following Kiniksa and Zura (10-27). Lilly's 39% revenue growth only lifted LLY about 4–5% premarket, below the 5% bar for big caps.
