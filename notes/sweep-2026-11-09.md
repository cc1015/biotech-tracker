## Patterns

- **Pharma is buying AI-native discovery platforms outright.** argenx–UNP is a platform buy (macrocyclic peptides plus machine learning), not an asset deal, and it follows two years as a partner and a stake taken in the Series B. In the same week Pierre Fabre signed XtalPi and Roche signed Chai (11-05). Mid-size pharma is now doing what big pharma did in 2025. Hot silos: `ai-discovery` and `small-molecule`.
- **RNA and in vivo cell engineering keep attracting seed and Series A money.** SRTD (cell-selective mRNA) and Afana (LNP in vivo CAR-T) are both academic spinouts: a Forschungszentrum Jülich group and a USTC professor team. Pharma venture arms (Boehringer, Lilly) are in SRTD's seed. Chinese in vivo CAR-T and CAR-NK companies (Afana, Rongu) are funded mostly by local state-linked funds.
- **Neuropsychiatry asset companies can raise again.** Delix is an asset-centric UC Davis-derived company with one lead program. Its round grew from a reported $85M to $100M, and J&J's corporate VC co-led. It is the clearest sign yet of a recovery in non-hallucinogenic psychedelic-inspired drugs.
- **Readouts drive the stock moves.** VIR rose ~15% on a clean pivotal readout in hepatitis delta, setting up a contest with Gilead. ZURA lost about three-quarters of its value on a Phase 2 miss caused by the high placebo response typical of HS trials. Survodutide clearing CVOT removes a key safety risk for glucagon-dual agonists, which keeps pressure on obesity M&A after AZ–Rhythm.
- **Tech entrants:** no new Alphabet/Isomorphic, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Apple, OpenAI or Anthropic life-sciences moves in the window. The Isomorphic ~$5B raise is still only talks. The Anthropic IPO is calendared for 11-12.
