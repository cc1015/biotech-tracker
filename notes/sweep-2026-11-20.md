## Patterns

- **In vivo cell therapy and China licensing keep converging.** Pfizer's first in vivo CAR-T deal pays $330M upfront for three pre-IND programs from Suzhou-based Starna, which makes them via targeted LNP-mRNA. That is a large upfront for preclinical assets. It continues the pattern of big pharma in-licensing early Chinese assets, seen earlier with Novartis–Abogen and Lilly–METiS.
- **Antibody-engineering platforms are still pulling in big headline deals at the research stage.** AbbVie–DISCO, at $40M upfront and up to $2.5B, is an option deal on AND-gated bispecifics built on surfaceome mapping. It follows Lilly–Stipple ($5.9B) and Novartis–OBI (>$2B) last sweep. AbbVie Ventures was already a seed investor in DISCO, so the deal turns a venture stake into a pipeline option.
- **Tools and automation are getting large rounds.** MegaRobo raised $200M for AI-plus-robotics labs, on sovereign and China-fund money, and Ansa raised $71.5M for enzymatic long-DNA synthesis, with Illumina Ventures joining. The lab-automation and synbio silos are active even while therapeutics headlines dominate.
- **How companies were built:** these were mostly founder-led or of unknown origin, not studio-built. Interlude is a single-asset revival: a family-founded company holding larazotide, whose earlier Phase 3 was dropped in 2022. VitriVax and Ansa are platforms. DISCO and Starna are platforms that make money through partnerships. No studio launches turned up in the window.
- **Regulatory:** two rare-disease new-drug approvals came early. Sanofi's Infilbray, the first therapy for neurological GD3, was approved a week before its 11-25 PDUFA date. Beren's Lembaz is the first NPC drug with a survival benefit; it carries a boxed warning for hearing loss and came with a priority review voucher.
- **Tech companies:** Microsoft is supplying Danaher with the agent layer (Azure plus Foundry), a month after Danaher partnered with NVIDIA. So instrument makers are working with more than one AI platform. Alphabet had nothing new after Isomorphic–MSD. No life-sciences moves turned up from NVIDIA, Amazon, Meta, OpenAI, Anthropic or Apple; Anthropic's Nasdaq debut on 11-19 is not a life-sciences event.
