## Patterns

- **UK academic spinouts lead big early rounds.** The window's two largest raises both came from Oxbridge labs: T-Therapeutics (Cambridge, Allan Bradley's humanized-mouse TCR platform) and Alethio (Oxford, Mead/Psaila MPN biology). Neither is studio-built. Both are platform-plus-lead-asset companies backed by university-linked funds (CIC, OSE, Cancer Research Horizons), and specialist US investors (RA Capital, Samsara) joined both.
- **Pharma venture arms are buying in before the clinic.** J&J, Lilly and Sanofi Ventures all sit in T-Therapeutics' preclinical cap table, and Lilly is also in Biograil's syndicate. Together with yesterday's Boehringer–Sino preclinical options, this shows pharma taking positions earlier in development.
- **Immunology licensing goes to oral, non-immunosuppressive mechanisms.** AbbVie bought an epithelium-acting EP4 agonist that Nxera had shelved, and Evommune licensed a Chinese VAV1 molecular glue degrader. China again supplied a `protein-degradation` asset under a ~$1B headline.
- **AI platforms keep signing pharma, but without disclosed money.** Chai has now announced collaborations with Lilly, Novartis, BMS, GSK and Roche in 2026, none with terms. Bloomberg (2026-11-04) reports that Isomorphic is in talks to raise about $5B at about $50B, up from the October talks at $40B+. That isn't recorded because no round has been announced.
- **Tech entrants:** no new Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Apple, NVIDIA, OpenAI or Anthropic life-sciences moves were found in the window. Anthropic's IPO is calendared for 2026-11-12. Its S-1 (2026-10-26) is already recorded.
