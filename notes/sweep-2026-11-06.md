## Patterns

- **AI drug discovery is moving to physics-based data generation.** Fathom (Anton-supercomputer alumni; simulation plus AI) and Potential Sciences (GPU CCSD(T) to generate training data for atomistic models) are both founder-led platforms with no lab or studio behind them. Both are bets that computed data, not more experimental data, is the bottleneck. A government sovereign-AI fund co-leading a drug-discovery round is new for this log.
- **"Spin out the leftovers" is now a standard way to start a company.** Anaheim Bio follows Setara (10-30): when a buyer takes only the lead asset (BioMarin–Alesta; Jazz–Actio), the same management and investors move the remaining programs into a new company and recapitalize it with a Series A. These are asset-centric companies with the original syndicate staying in.
- **Pressure on obesity and metabolic M&A keeps building.** The AstraZeneca–Rhythm approach came days after Rhythm's LG Chem licence was reworked, and analysts expect a bidding war. Hot silos this week: `small-molecule` (obesity, CPPD, rare metabolic) and `ai-discovery`.
- **Late-stage rare-disease companies are cutting gene-therapy spending.** Ultragenyx ended an AAV program (UX701) and an ASO program (GTX-102) and is cutting R&D in half to reach profitability. Sanofi's early exit from a `protein-degradation` asset two months into Phase 1 is a reminder of how much partnered degrader value depends on the partner.
- **Tech entrants:** no new Alphabet/Isomorphic, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Apple, OpenAI or Anthropic life-sciences moves in the window. The Isomorphic ~$5B raise (Bloomberg) is still only talks. The Anthropic IPO is calendared for 2026-11-12.
