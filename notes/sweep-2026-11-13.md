## Patterns

- **Private capital is paying for late-stage obesity trials.** Blackstone's $1.5B for berobenatide is synthetic-royalty-style R&D funding. Pfizer keeps the asset and shifts Phase 3 cost risk to Blackstone, after an earlier $326M Pfizer–Blackstone deal.
- **Public companies are raising on good news.** Vir ($150M, after pivotal HDV data), INOVIO ($75M, after its first DNA-medicine approval) and Windward (IPO priced at the midpoint, with a strategic pharma placement) all sold stock right after a catalyst. Vir's ~17.5% discount to the prior close shows how much the window costs. Windward is the third biotech IPO in three weeks, after Lycia and Bambusa.
- **Assets licensed from China keep reaching public markets.** Windward was built around antibodies licensed from China (WIN378, anti-TSLP) and is backed by Sanofi. This is the same single-asset in-licensing model as the Corxel SPAC and last week's Vor–RemeGen licence.
- **Academic cell-therapy assets are changing hands cheaply.** NeuroAirmid had no employees and is funded by a CIRM grant, and it sold for under $7M up front. Its main value is HUNT1's milestones and the clinical trial CIRM pays for. This is an academic spinout handing its programme to a small-cap platform company, not a venture-scale exit.
- **AI for biology outside drugs is getting funded.** Biomatter, an enzyme-design platform with paying customers (Thermo Fisher, AZ, Sanofi, BASF), raised an $18M Series A. It's another European platform seed/A, like Orakl and Neobe earlier this week.
- **Tools consolidation continues, and it's about proteomics.** Waters–MOBILion follows MaxCyte–Akron (11-10). Large instrument makers are buying separation and sample-prep technology to compete in proteomics.
- **Tech companies:** no new life-sciences moves from Alphabet/Isomorphic/DeepMind, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, OpenAI, Anthropic or Apple in the window. Off-topic but relevant to the `tech_entrants` watchlist: NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang resigned on 11-12, and CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. That could affect NVIDIA's BioNeMo/NVentures activity. Anthropic's IPO is still expected around 11-19.
