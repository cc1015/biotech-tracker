## Patterns

- **ADCs dominated ESMO.** Of the 14 clinical rows, 8 are ADCs: sac-TMT, Sone-Ve, Ris-Rez, arcotatug tavatecan, iza-bren, trastuzumab pamirtecan, Rina-S and the VOLGA EV combination. Most of these assets were licensed from Chinese companies: Kelun, KYM, Hansoh, Innovent, SystImmune/Biokin and DualityBio. China-built ADC platforms are now producing global Phase 3 wins for big pharma. The exceptions are BioNTech/DualityBio, where the companies themselves call the benefit limited, and Sone-Ve's PFS miss. Zai Lab's $250M PIPE for a DLL3 ADC fits the same theme.
- **China assets keep moving West, and the deal forms are changing.** Gilead bought PepStar's IL-23R pill outright instead of licensing it. Corxel (formerly Ji Xing) is taking a Vincentage-licensed oral GLP-1 public through a SPAC, and Windward Bio filed for an IPO built on China-derived assets. This came in the same week Treasury proposed COINS Act rules on China biotech investment that reportedly spare licensing deals.
- **Reverse mergers and SPACs are carrying private asset companies to market.** Bolt/Prana (a single-asset Phase 2 bispecific, founder-led by Sheila Gujrathi) and Corxel/Amanat (a single asset) both pair a listing with a large crossover PIPE. Bolt rose about 69% on the day it announced. The same crossover funds (RA Capital, RTW, TCGX, ADAR1) appear in the Corxel, Zai Lab and Bolt PIPEs.
- **Seed and early money is going to tools and devices, not drugs.** DropGenie (microfluidic gene editing, McGill-linked, founder-led) and Dragonfleye (an ocular delivery device built on a surgeon's clinical research) are both platform plays. The window had no new studio launches from Flagship, ARCH, Third Rock, Atlas or Foresite.
- **Tech companies:** Anthropic's S-1 is the only tech-company move in the window. Per coverage, it presents life sciences as a business line, citing a BSL-1/2 wet lab, the Coefficient Bio purchase and Novo and Lilly partnerships, and it flags biosecurity and lab-liability risks. Nothing new from Alphabet/Isomorphic, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, OpenAI or Apple after last week's RFD4 and Orchestra items.
