## Patterns

- **AI drug discovery is being built as services, and as platforms grown from data.** Warp Sciences ($215M at $865M) runs a robotic chemistry CRO and has no pipeline of its own. It is founder-led by an ex-OpenAI executive and backed by tech VCs (Lightspeed, Lux, Conviction). Reflector Bio is an academic spinout from the Theis lab (Helmholtz Munich) that sells a phenotypic foundation model. Pathos ($237M at $3B) is a hybrid: an AI platform plus in-licensed Chinese and AZ assets. Its round was led strategically by BMS rather than by a VC.
- **ADCs stay the hottest modality for new money.** Hexagon (natural-product payloads, Column Group), Trior (Korean masking and linker platform) and Pathos' lead JSKN016 are all ADC bets. Each is built around a platform, with a lead asset.
- **Late-stage readouts drove the biggest stock moves.** EyePoint fell 85% and cut 53% of staff after a Phase 3 miss. Arcus fell 20% on data plus dilution. Biohaven rose about 33% when the FDA accepted its resubmission. Among wins with a large market, AbbVie (lutikizumab) and Merck/Moderna (intismeran) both delivered, but with smaller effects than hoped.
- **Tech companies:** Alphabet's Isomorphic signed a government AI-discovery MoU in Abu Dhabi. OpenAI's Orchestra deal (10-20) and NVIDIA's RFD4 release (10-21) were already logged. Nothing new from Microsoft, Meta, Amazon, Apple or Anthropic in the window. Anthropic appears only indirectly, via a Bloomberg item on Citrini Research using Claude Science.
- **Security incidents in life sciences keep coming.** The Quest Quanum breach follows earlier 2026 attacks on Stryker, Medtronic and others, per MedTech Dive.
