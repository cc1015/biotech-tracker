## Patterns

- **Computational platforms are moving from small molecules into biologics.** Schrödinger's first acquisition in four years buys antibody-repertoire analytics (ENPICOM) to feed its physics-based design tools. It follows last week's Boltz $110M and Isomorphic $5B rounds. The money in `ai-discovery` is going to platforms that cover the whole stack, from sequence to optimized molecule.
- **Reverse mergers are still the route to Nasdaq for platform biotechs.** Remix (Atlas-built; small molecules that reprogram RNA processing) used a cash-poor Penn gene-therapy shell, Passage Bio. It follows Avenzo–Rallybio (11-20) and Clywedog–Barinthus. Each brought a $100–215M PIPE in place of an IPO, and the shells came from academic-spinout gene-therapy companies that ran out of road.
- **RNA delivery and circRNA are moving toward in vivo cell therapy.** CirCode (Shanghai) pairs circRNA protein replacement for ischemia with in vivo CAR-T and CAR-M programs. That's the same convergence seen at Afana and Orbital. China-built platforms in `rna` and `cell-therapy` keep raising money from domestic strategics (Fosun).
- **Hong Kong is the radiopharma IPO venue.** Sinotau is HK's first Chapter 18A radiopharma listing. Retail demand was enormous (5,229x), but the stock still opened below an offer priced at the bottom of its range. `radiopharma` follows Eli Radiopharma's RMB400M Series A (11-11).
- **Big pharma is extending its biologics with less frequent dosing.** GSK's twice-yearly depemokimab matched monthly Nucala in EGPA. It's a lifecycle play rather than a new mechanism, and it comes while Nucala itself faces erosion.
- **Tech companies:** no confirmed new moves in the window. Search surfaced only older Amazon, OpenAI and NVIDIA items, plus an 11-19 "Anthropic life-sciences push" mentioned in one secondary roundup (Life Science Leader). That item isn't in `news.csv` and has no primary source yet, so it's listed as a follow-up below.
