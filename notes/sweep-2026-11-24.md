## Patterns

- **Big pharma is buying commercial-stage respiratory and immunology assets.** Sanofi–Insmed is 2026's largest pharma deal per BioSpace and the first large move under CEO Belén Garijo. It buys a drug that is already a blockbuster (Brinsupri, DPP1, bronchiectasis) to offset the Dupixent patent cliff. It follows a run of preclinical buys (Lilly–LoQus23, Lilly–Calytrix) and mid-stage ones (J&J–Ollin). Pharma is shopping at every stage, but the big money went to revenue.
- **Protein degradation is hot again.** KT-621 shows an oral pill can match antibodies against the IL-4/IL-13 pathway (mid dose: EASI-75 62% vs 22%). Kymera raised $1.75B the same night, priced at the day's close and upsized from $1.5B. Kymera is a platform company Atlas built in 2016, and this round validates that platform model. It also points to oral drugs that mimic biologics as a theme for immunology in 2027.
- **Oncology approvals are arriving early.** Wilserdly got its second FDA approval in eight days, ahead of its ~11-30 goal date, as the first new adjuvant endocrine therapy in two decades. BridgeBio's Welgemda also came before its 11-27 date (not recorded; see below). That continues last week's pattern of approvals on or before PDUFA.
- **Small, data-first AI seeds keep coming.** Leash (founder-led by ex-Recursion staff) is raising small rounds to build its own DNA-encoded-library data instead of training on public data. That's the reverse of Isomorphic's $5B round last week. Apiary is a UBC/CCRM academic spinout in a new cell-therapy sub-wave: engineered B cells that make proteins in the body.
- **Reverse mergers keep taking the place of IPOs.** Clywedog–Barinthus closed with only ~$35M of cash, compared with Avenzo's $215M PIPE last week. One more Oxford spinout (Vaccitech) has left the tracked public list.
- **Tech companies:** no confirmed new life-sciences moves in the window. Searches surfaced only older items: Danaher–Microsoft (11-20, already recorded), Amazon Bio Discovery and Anthropic's October drug-development post.
