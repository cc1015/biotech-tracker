## Patterns

- **Pharma keeps buying preclinical neuro and heme assets outright.** Lilly–LoQus23 is Lilly's 15th acquisition of 2026 per BioSpace, and its second preclinical buy in a week after Calytrix. It pays up to $725M for an oral MSH3 inhibitor targeting Huntington's somatic repeat expansion. LoQus23 was studio-like in origin: entrepreneurs-in-residence at SV Health's Dementia Discovery Fund founded it, then Novartis Venture Fund and Forbion backed it. That route from an investor's in-house incubation to a quick pharma exit keeps recurring.
- **The siRNA/RNA silo had its biggest week of the quarter.** ORION-4 is the first cardiovascular-outcomes win for an siRNA, and Livsayis is the first siRNA approved in gMG. Both came from Alnylam-discovered molecules, now owned by Novartis and Regeneron. Together they support the long-acting, rarely dosed RNA model in common diseases.
- **Experienced-founder and reverse-merger financings are filling the gap left by IPOs.** Ritzey raised $112.5M with a founder-led, asset-centric plan: Sharon Mates of Intra-Cellular buying Perha's DYRK1A program. Avenzo, an in-licensing platform with CDK2/4 inhibitors and bispecific ADCs, went public through Rallybio's cash shell with a $215M PIPE rather than an IPO.
- **Regulatory risk is the dominant stock story.** Capricor's second CRL cited data integrity, not just efficacy, and the shares lost ~75%. This follows Summit's CRL last week. Meanwhile Nelkarmi, Livsayis, Klygefa, Infilbray and Lembaz were all approved on or before their goal dates, so FDA timeliness held even as it rejected weaker packages.
- **Builders and services:** Flagship's £100M London Origination Lab is its first company-creation unit outside the US, aiming at AI-native platforms built around NHS data. IQVIA buying Quotient continues the CRO push into early development and manufacturing, after its Charles River assets deal in February.
- **Tech companies:** no confirmed new life-sciences moves in the window. OpenAI's MedChemBench was reported on 11-20 but couldn't be verified at a primary source. Danaher–Microsoft (11-20) was recorded last sweep.
