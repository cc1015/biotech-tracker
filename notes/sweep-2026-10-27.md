## Patterns
- **Allogeneic cell therapy is taking hits.** Artiva is ending its autoimmune NK program, including a planned Phase 3. Safety (fludarabine conditioning) and durability are the stated reasons. It follows Caribou's shutdown (10-07, lymphoma CAR-T; STAT), so two off-the-shelf cell-therapy companies have folded this month. It is left with ~$319M of cash on a wind-down track, which makes it a likely reverse-merger shell.
- **Antibodies remain the most licensed modality,** and Korean buyers are now active. Celltrion's third Kaigene asset continues a pattern of Asian pharma licensing early US autoimmune antibodies rather than the usual China-outbound direction.
- **Big pharma Phase 3s are bispecific/ADC heavy:** Elrexfio (BCMA×CD3) joins the run of ESMO-week ADC and bispecific readouts.
- **Tech companies are moving into clinical operations rather than discovery.** Microsoft's Boehringer deal (synthetic control arms) follows OpenAI–Orchestra Bio (10-20) and Anthropic's S-1 life-sciences pitch (10-26). AI is being sold for trial design and operations as much as for molecule design.
- **IPO window:** Lycia (academic spinout, Bertozzi/Stanford, platform) closed at ~$365M with the full greenshoe but fell ~10% after hours. Platform degrader stories can still raise big amounts, but after-market support is thin.
